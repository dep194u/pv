Energy management summary

  1. The AI models estimate the load and PV output for each house.
  2. Houses with extra PV energy are matched with houses having an energy deficit.
  3. After P2P matching, the shared battery absorbs remaining surplus or supports remaining deficit, subject to limits.
  4. Any remaining deficit is imported from the grid; any remaining surplus is exported.
